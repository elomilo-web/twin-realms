TWIN REALMS - BETA 1.3  (Windows, 64-bit)
==========================================

START
  Double-click TwinRealms.exe. Nothing to install.
  Keep everything in this folder together: the .exe needs TwinRealms.pck and the data_TwinRealms_windows_x86_64 folder.
  If Windows says "Windows protected your PC": click "More info", then "Run anyway" (the game is not signed yet).

HEROES
  The title screen has 3 hero slots. Pick an empty slot to create a hero (race, class, name), or press Play on one you made.
  Esc > Quit to title saves and takes you back to the heroes.
  Saves live in %APPDATA%\Godot\app_userdata\TwinRealms\ (save.json holds all 3 heroes). Deleting the game folder keeps them.

CONTROLS (change any of them in Settings > Controls)
  WASD move, Shift sprint, Space jump, C roll
  Left mouse attack (hold to keep your chain going), Right mouse heavy attack, F guard with a shield, O basic attack chains
  1-6 skills, H health potion, G mana potion, E interact, R shift between Light and Shadow, T horse
  Tab lock on (again: next target), Q release lock-on
  I pack, J journal, M map, K skill tree, L skills, U modifier tree, Esc menu
  F8 flag a moment for review (screenshot + report in %APPDATA%\Godot\app_userdata\TwinRealms\playtest\)

PACK (I)
  Click an item to equip it. Right-click to drop it on the ground (you or a friend can pick it up again).
  Disassemble: break items into scraps.  Crafting: make gear, elemental gear, set pieces and potions from scraps.
  Pickup filter: choose which items you pick up (the rest stays on the ground, or is scrapped).
  Blacksmith: hone gear up to +10, reroll its bonus stats, rename it or pick its glow colour.

SKILLS AND ATTRIBUTES
  Right-click a learned skill (in the skill list or on the hotbar) to take back a rank and get the point back.
  Esc > Character > Reset attributes returns every spent attribute point.

PLAY WITH A FRIEND
  Both of you need this same version.
  Host: Multiplayer > Host a game (allow Twin Realms through the Windows firewall when asked).
        Then Esc > Multiplayer and press Copy next to a join code. Send it to your friend.
        "Over the internet" code: friends anywhere.  "Same Wi-Fi / VPN" code: friends in your house.
  Friend: Multiplayer > Paste > Join. Building the world takes a little while after joining; that is normal.
  If the host's menu says the router did not open the port: forward UDP port 24565 to the host's computer,
  or both install Tailscale or Radmin VPN (free) and use the "Same Wi-Fi / VPN" code.
  Walk up to your friend and press E to invite them to a party: a party shares dungeons, experience and gold.
  Monsters are 1.5x tougher while two or more heroes share the world.

LEVELLING
  Each level gives 2 attribute points (Esc > Character), 1 skill point (L) and 2 skill tree points (K).
  Monsters keep up with you: overworld monsters rise to your level, and the longer you play in one sitting, the stronger they get.
  Dungeon monsters are your level + 2 (a party's average level + 3), tougher on every floor and much tougher every 5 floors.
  Deeper floors drop rarer loot; set gear drops from floor 10 down.

FOUND A BUG?
  Press F8 when it happens, then tell us what you did. The game's log is in %APPDATA%\Godot\app_userdata\TwinRealms\logs\
