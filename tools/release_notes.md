## Twin Realms 1.1 beta (Windows)

**Download `TwinRealms-1.1-beta-Windows.zip` below, extract it and run `TwinRealms.exe`.** If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click **More info** > **Run anyway**. Needs Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) and a DirectX 12 graphics card. `HOW_TO_PLAY.txt` is inside the zip.

Multiplayer: both players need 1.1 (1.0 cannot join 1.1 games). **Multiplayer is unreliable in this build** (a joining friend's moves and hits may not reach the host); it is being fixed.

### New since 1.0 beta
- **A much bigger world.** The old map is now the Verdant Isle. Five new lands lie across the sea, joined by long stone bridges: Frostspire Reach (snow and pine), Sunscar Expanse (desert and mesas), Mirefen Marshes (swamp and stilt villages), Emberwild Highlands (autumn forest and a volcano) and Elderwood Vale (giant trees). They hold walled cities, castles, villages, ruins, tombs, caves, rivers and lakes. Eight floating sky islands are reached by riding updrafts. No monsters out there yet.
- **New look.** Textured ground with grass, dirt, gravel, rock and snow, dense wind-swept grass, varied trees, warmer sunlight, bounce lighting, a dusty-blue night and no cartoon outlines.
- **Graphics settings.** Quality (Low to Ultra), view distance and grass amount under Settings > Display.
- **Combat feel.** Swings step in and connect, enemy blows land properly, and only special abilities stagger you (ordinary hits no longer interrupt you). A skill that gets interrupted is refunded and has no cooldown. Feet stay planted on slopes and while running.
- **Sound.** Footsteps that change with the ground. Separate volume sliders for music, combat, footsteps and the interface. The noisy bird ambience and the guitar song are gone.
- **Menus.** A skill grid with search and filters, a new Modifier Tree (U) for all skill and attack modifiers, a skill tree with search and click-to-take paths, and a clearer character sheet.
- **Combat systems.** Basic attack chains per weapon (O), a heavy attack (right mouse), hold to aim area spells and to charge empowered spells. Wave arena valor and the Valor Quartermaster.

### Known limits
- Multiplayer: a joining friend's position and hits may not reach the host.
- No swimming yet: use the bridges.
- Cave mouths have no inside yet.
- The new lands are empty of monsters for now.
