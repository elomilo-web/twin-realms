## Twin Realms 2.2

**Install: download `TwinRealmsSetup.exe` below and run it.** If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click **More info** > **Run anyway**. Needs Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) and a DirectX 12 graphics card. Already playing? Just start the game: the launcher updates itself to 2.2. Your heroes carry over.

### Combat, Souls-style
- Swept weapon hitboxes: a blow lands where the blade actually goes, for you and for monsters.
- Poise, stagger, guard break, **parry and riposte**, **backstab**, roll invincibility and a backstep. Heavy blows knock you down; you get back up (untouchable while rising).
- Swings are committed and keep their spacing: the hero stays at the weapon's reach so the blade, not the fist, meets the foe. No more sliding across the ground while attacking.
- Weapons are held on the handle and drawn at believable sizes. With a free off hand, swords are swung with **both hands**.
- A real tucked **roll**, a held **block** that takes the blow, a heavy attack that winds up the moment you press, arms that no longer stick out in a T when jumping, smoother sword trails.
- 12 new weapon classes, colossal swords / axes / hammers, greatshields, item sizes, **dual wielding**, durability with smelting and repair, 25 new weapon models and 26 named finds.

### Heroes
- 5 new classes (Monk, Bard, Shaman, Samurai, Chronomancer) and 5 new races (Beastkin, Fae, Undying, Tidekin, Oni).
- A character creator, new hair styles, faces and ears. Transformations, 10 new skill tree branches and an advanced tree.
- New stats: Wisdom, Agility, Luck, Endurance. Gems with sockets, fusing and gem dust.

### The world
- About 100 NPCs with homes, daily schedules and personalities who talk, argue, trade and remember you. Reputation changes prices and greetings.
- 32 story quests with choices and consequences. Crime and the town watch.
- Swimming and diving, boats, fishing, cooking, farming and ranching, 17 life skills, sneaking and lockpicking.
- Pets: catch any monster in a Soul Cube, level them, ride them, duel them, trade them.
- Breakable crates, barrels and pots, up to 232 new places to find, block-built towns and castles, doors, chests and torches.
- Seven new songs and playable instruments (lute, flute, drum, harp).

### Online
- Chat channels, friends, player trading, party quest sharing, an auction house.

### Interface
- Compact menus, first-person view (F5), a command bar, hotbar names and tooltips, new skill icons and custom spell icons.
