## Twin Realms 2.1

**Install: download `TwinRealmsSetup.exe` below and run it.** If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click **More info** > **Run anyway**. Needs Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) and a DirectX 12 graphics card. Already playing? Just start the game: the launcher updates itself to 2.1. Your heroes carry over.

### Combat
- Fixed: after the first attack, basic attacks never landed (wands and staves fired nothing, melee did no damage). Every swing hits again.
- Wand bolts fly as bright comets with a muzzle flash; staff orbs flash too.
- Fighting with no weapon is now **Fist Forms**: Jab, Cross, Hook (Flurry and Haymaker unlock), with fist sounds.
- Echo and Resonance no longer give free casts: each repeat restarts the cooldown. Auto-cast supports respect cooldowns.
- The dodge roll faces the way you roll.

### The world
- **Level zones**: monsters no longer copy your level. Each region has its own level range (Oakhaven Meadows 1-5 up to the Elderwood 50-55); higher zones give more XP, gold and better loot. A banner shows the zone when you enter.
- Monsters come in **packs** with a stronger leader; packmates join the fight. They notice you from closer and give up when pulled too far from camp.
- Dungeon and boss scaling rebalanced: bosses take minutes, not thousands of hits. Dungeons are much brighter.
- Scarletta's lair: the floor and Scarletta are lit, and the walk to her is about half as long.
- Sky islands render properly; characters no longer sink into the block ground.
- The arena is rebuilt as a colosseum with tiered stands and spectators.

### Building
- **Pickaxes** (Wooden, Stone, Iron, Runed) dig faster and mine harder blocks, held in your hand in build mode.
- A **block hotbar** (1-9, mouse wheel, D-pad) and new blocks: torches that light up, glass, stairs, slabs and fences.

### Gear and spells
- 10 new layered armor pieces (chainmail, surcoat, coat, gauntlets, greaves, tassets...) that fit every body, 18 new weapons and 4 new helmets; helmets fit properly. Steel looks like steel; rarity shows as a subtle rim.
- Golden Wings look right in flight.
- **Spellcraft**: spells chain up to 3 sections (section III opens at level 12), with an easier layout.

### Interface and controls
- **Controller support** (Xbox and PlayStation): every menu works with the pad, button prompts match your controller, rebind in Settings > Controller.
- Menus never stretch off screen: long lists scroll. A new compact inventory.
- **Loading screens** with tips instead of frozen screens.

### Performance
- Big frame-rate gains: an **Auto** quality setting picks the right preset for your screen (FSR upscaling on high resolutions), compressed textures, and far-away monsters cost nothing. Smoother animation.

### Removing the game
Windows Settings > Apps > Installed apps > **Twin Realms** > Uninstall. You choose whether to keep your offline heroes.
