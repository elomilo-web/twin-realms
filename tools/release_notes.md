## Twin Realms 1.0

**Install: download `TwinRealmsSetup.exe` below and run it.** It installs Twin Realms and its launcher, adds shortcuts, and starts the game. If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click **More info** > **Run anyway**. Needs Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) and a DirectX 12 graphics card. Your heroes from the beta carry over.

Already playing the 1.4 beta? Just start it: it updates itself to 1.0.

### The launcher
- Every start opens the launcher first. It checks for updates and installs them for you, with progress bars.
- **Patch notes** for the newest version, right in the launcher.
- **Log in** to your online account with **Remember me** and **Log in automatically**.
- **Play Online** takes you straight to your online heroes; **Play Offline** opens your heroes on this computer and Multiplayer.

### Twin Realms Online
- One shared world with everyone online. Create an account with your email and a password.
- Your online heroes are saved on the server, so you can play them from any computer.

### A darker world
- Gothic art overhaul: a weathered village, muted colours, dimmer spell effects and new materials.
- Painted textures on the village, castle and dungeon buildings, new skill and item icons, new menu and title art.
- Macros and auto potions.

### Removing the game
Windows Settings > Apps > Installed apps > **Twin Realms** > Uninstall. You choose whether to keep your offline heroes.
