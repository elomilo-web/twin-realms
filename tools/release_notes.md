## Twin Realms 1.4 beta (Windows)

**Download the zip below, extract it and run `TwinRealms.exe`.** If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click **More info** > **Run anyway**. Needs Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) and a DirectX 12 graphics card. Your heroes from earlier versions carry over.

**This is the last download you need by hand.** From 1.4 on, the game checks for updates every time it starts and installs them itself before you play.

### Twin Realms Online
- **Play Online** on the title screen: create an account with email and password and play in one shared world with everyone online.
- Your online heroes are saved on the server, so you can play them from any computer.
- Play Online unlocks once the game is up to date. Offline play and Multiplayer (hosting friends) work as before.

### A darker world
- Gothic art overhaul: a weathered village, muted colours, dimmer spell effects and new materials.
- Painted textures on the village, castle and dungeon buildings, new skill and item icons, new menu art and title art.
- Macros and auto potions.

Multiplayer: everyone needs 1.4. Older versions cannot join 1.4 games or the online server.
