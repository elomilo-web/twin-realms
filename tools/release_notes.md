## Twin Realms 1.3 beta (Windows)

**Download `TwinRealms-1.3-beta-Windows.zip` below, extract it and run `TwinRealms.exe`.** If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click **More info** > **Run anyway**. Needs Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) and a DirectX 12 graphics card. `HOW_TO_PLAY.txt` is inside the zip. Your heroes from 1.2 carry over.

Multiplayer: everyone needs this build of 1.3 (1.2, and 1.3 downloaded before November 14, cannot join its games).

**No more maxed-out CPU on first launch.** Earlier builds made every player's PC compile all of the game's shaders on its first run, pinning every core. 1.3 ships them pre-compiled. The game also caps its frame rate at 144 by default and drops to 15 frames a second while it is in the background.

### First raid: Scarletta, The Rotting Blade
- **Enter from the shrine on Lumen Aerie**, the floating island above Oakhaven, alone or with your party.
- **The Rotting Hollow:** halls haunted by **black shades of your own party**, then a kilometre-wide chamber of giant glowing mushrooms and slime lakes, with its own music.
- **Scarletta** reads her challengers and adapts her defences and tactics to them. She has several health bars, sweeping attacks, feints, long tracking wind-ups and gap closers that always reach you. She may also blink behind you and grab you.
- **The Bloom:** at 75%, 50% and 25% health, deal enough damage in time or a rot wave poisons everyone. Poison heals her.
- **Raid rules:** the fallen stay down until a party member revives them, with the Resurrection spell or by sharing half their health. Monsters target the closest player unless you deal enough damage, taunt, or she picks someone at random.
- **Rewards:** a personal chest for every challenger (rare loot and **Golden Wings**, which let you fly), about three levels of experience, and the title **Felled Angel** (+15% movement speed and a trail of white feathers).
- Shades drop **white angel feathers**, potions, gold and the new **katana** weapon type.

### Gear and the blacksmith
- **Bonus stats:** epic gear rolls 2-4 and legendary gear 3-5 bonus stats from 55 kinds: damage of every type, crit, penetration, speeds, cooldowns, health, mana, defences, resistances, regen, leech, experience, gold find and luck. Gear glows brighter the more bonus stats it has.
- **Luck** brings more and rarer drops, and it is pooled across your party.
- **The blacksmith:** hone any piece up to +10, reroll its bonus stats, rename it and choose its glow colour. Trade any 10 of one material for 1 of another.
- **Five forged sets** that never drop: Seraph's Reverie, Stormcaller's Mantle, Bulwark of the Deep, Ember Revenant and Nightglass Raiment. Make them at Crafting > Set pieces.

### Skills
- **Respec:** right-click a learned skill to take back a rank and get the point back. Character > **Reset attributes** returns every attribute point.
- **41 new skills**, with new kinds: boomerangs, orbiting blades, turrets, beams, vortexes, delayed marks, walls, teleports, traps, shields and hooks. **Phantom Rush** is a cheap gap closer that leaves afterimages.
- **10 enchanter auras** buff your whole party: damage, shields, speed, healing, cleansing, immunities and more.
- **Challenging Roar** (taunt) and **Resurrection** are new.

### World and interface
- **Dungeon minimap** that fills in as you explore. Party members show on the minimap and the map. The map zooms and pans.
- **Skill tree:** you can spend a point on another class's starting node to build a hybrid across the centre.
- **Titles** can be worn from the Character menu.

### Multiplayer
- **Everyone now sees the same monsters.** Friends used to see stale or missing monsters away from the host.

### Fixes
- The Shadow Duel's Shadow is twice the hero's size, and visible again.
- Dying a second time no longer respawns you instantly.
- Skills no longer stop working until a restart after a whirlwind or leap is interrupted (melee and berserker).
